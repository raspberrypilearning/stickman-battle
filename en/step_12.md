## Challenge: a two-player battle

Turn Stickman Battle into a game for **two players**, with one fighter each.

> [!CHALLENGE]
>
> + Can you give the fighter a rival by making a second stickman that already knows how to punch, kick, and slash?
> + Can you make the two fighters easy to tell apart by giving each one its own strike colour on its fists?
> + Can you set the second fighter up on the other side of the stage, facing its opponent, the moment the green flag is clicked?
> + Can you make each fighter feel a hit by sensing the *other* fighter's strike colour?
> + Can you say goodbye to the enemy sprite and build a proper two-player fight?

> [!HINT]
>
> Duplicate the **player** sprite so the second fighter starts with the same costumes, sounds, and scripts.
>
> ![Duplicating a sprite by right-clicking its thumbnail.](images/sprite-duplicate.png)

> [!HINT]
>
> Recolour one fighter's punch, kick, and slash costumes. Each fighter needs a different strike colour.

> [!HINT]
>
> Start the fighters on opposite sides of the stage and point them towards each other.

> [!HINT]
>
> Make each fighter sense the other fighter's strike colour. Keep both colours out of the backdrop so they only count as attacks.

> [!HINT]
>
> Delete the **enemy** sprite. Give the second fighter different controls, its own health, and a way to win when the other fighter's health reaches zero.

Change one thing at a time and test after each change.

> [!SAVE]
